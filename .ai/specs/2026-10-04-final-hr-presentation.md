## Story in ten slides

1. Laya Sec Layer: who acts, on which data, what the action does, and how much it uses.
2. Primary HR story: synthetic candidate record, scoped read/summary, human review of
   a proposed message. Explain the user benefit in plain language.
3. Graphical architecture: one identity/policy/audit, LLM boundary and MCP boundary,
   private model route, existing IAM and telemetry integration contracts. Visually
   distinguish implemented local services from external integration targets.
4. Human and agent authority: relational intersection, same-tenant separation,
   short-lived delegation, trusted identity provenance and stable budget ownership.
5. Tool catalog and exact consent: read/write/destructive, classes/reversibility,
   explainable heuristic, precise approval/resume and one actual local effect.
6. Threat ladder L0–L5 and the exact seven layers as separate axes. Show partial
   defenses/gaps accurately; live sophistication remains unknown unless a later
   accepted contract adds actual trusted classification.
7. Security/management operations: actual audit/collector state and measured per-
   department usage, explicit simulated tariff and unknown outcomes. Keep Dev and
   Finance examples to one sentence each and mark absent adapters honestly.
8. RODO, AI Act, DORA and OWASP: technical controls and evidence support specific
   obligations; no claim of automatic legal compliance or certification.
9. Evidence: unit, functional, integration and installed E2E, exact tested revision
   and a small understandable sample of actual zero-effect/one-effect assertions.
   Include material measured semantic limitations; tests are not a detector accuracy
   score. Avoid adding different overlapping test totals into one misleading number.
10. Run/demo/repository and next deployment boundary: simple actual install command
    with local-console mode, public repository link, implemented scope and the real
    external integration work that remains. No claim that existing bank systems have
    already been connected or that sending to the local collector is vendor delivery.

This order may be adjusted for readability while retaining all requested coverage.
No dense wall of text: cut repetition before shrinking fonts. Use editable source
diagrams and real architecture/evidence tables; no decorative fake dashboard metrics.
Keep technical construction/validation chatter outside slide content.
